## 🚀 Key Feature Highlights

### 🎨 1. 

NovaColor Studio (20,422+ Unlocked Swatches)
* **100% Copyright-Free & Unrestricted:** All **20,422** curated designer colors completely unlocked with zero paywalls or copyright locks.
* **Smart Sections & Sorting:** Browse **All Colors**, **Hue Families**, **Light/Dark**, and **Chromatic Rainbow** ordering.


* **Instant Export & Copy:** Dedicated 1-click copy pills for **Color Code, HEX, RGB & CMYK**, plus an interactive Palette Bucket drawer with instant `.PNG` and `.ACO` export.

### 🎛 2. 

Quick Control Center & Dynamic Ear Shield
* **Direct Audio Output Switcher:** Instant 1-click routing between Headphones, Studio Monitors, and Soundcards.
* **Master Volume & Mic Gain:** Precision sliders with rapid snap presets (`25%`, `50%`, `75%`, `100%`) and hardware DDC/CI monitor brightness & contrast control.


* **Dynamic Ear Shield (Sound Lock):** Zero-latency real-time WASAPI peak limiter protecting your ears by clamping gunshots, flashbangs, and screeching audio while keeping footsteps and soft speech crisp.
* **Windows Quick Toggles:** 1-click Flush DNS, Network Adapters, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth controls.



### 📷 3. NovaCam — 4K Smartphone-to-PC Hardware Webcam
* **Zero-Lag Camera Pipeline:** Turn your smartphone into an ultra-high-definition PC webcam over USB (ADB port `8088`) or Wi-Fi with sub-20ms latency.


* **100% Physical Emulation:** Registered as **"NovaDeck 4K Cam"** in Windows DirectShow and Media Foundation—works seamlessly on Discord, Google Meet, Zoom, and OBS.
* **On-the-Fly Controls:** In-app Digital Zoom, Exposure, Manual Focus, Flashlight toggle, Lens switcher, and rotation.



### 🌐 4. Network Studio & Multi-Line Bonding
* **Bandwidth Telemetry:** Real-time upload, download, and ping metrics with distinct per-adapter telemetry.


* **System-Wide Line Bonding:** Aggregates bandwidth across Ethernet, Wi-Fi, and 4G/5G USB mobile tethering simultaneously for maximum speed and instant failover protection.

### 📥 5. 

Universal 16-Thread Media Downloader
* **Multi-Platform Extractor:** One-click downloads for YouTube (Shorts, Playlists & 4K 60FPS Video), Instagram Reels, TikTok, Twitter/X, Facebook, and Reddit powered by `yt-dlp` and `ffmpeg`.
* **Concurrent Chunking & Audio:** Splits downloads into 16 parallel fragments and converts to 320kbps MP3 with automated album art and ID3 metadata.



### 🔊 6. Soundboard & Dual-Output Broadcast Player
* **Simultaneous Audio Ingestion:** Plays custom sound triggers in your headphones while broadcasting live into Discord, in-game voice chat, and OBS.


* **Preloaded Soundpack:** Comes with the curated NovaDeck starter soundpack, drag-and-drop MP3/WAV/OGG slots, and custom hotkey bindings.

### 🧹 7. 

Deep System Cleaner, RAM Booster & File Shredder
* **App-Aware Deep Cache Purge:** Cleans **WhatsApp Desktop, Telegram, Discord, Teams/Slack, Spotify, CapCut, OBS, Steam, Epic, Riot, Battle.net, VS Code, Cursor, npm, pip, NuGet**, `%TEMP%`, Windows Update leftovers, browser caches, and the **Windows Recycle Bin** with real app icons and instant RAM boost.

### 🛡 8. Firewall Guard, RGB Sync & Windows Deep Repair
